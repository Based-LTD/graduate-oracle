How to get started

  1. Open @graduate_oracle_bot in Telegram and send /start.
  2. Send /trader. Read the Terms of Service, tap I Accept.
  3. Tap 💰 Wallet — copy your Solana deposit address.
  4. Send SOL from Phantom (or anywhere) to that address. ~10 seconds to confirm.
  5. Tap 💸 Withdraw → set a withdraw password (8+ chars, save it somewhere safe).
  6. Tap ⚙️ Settings → 🎨 Strategy — pick Conservative / Balanced / YOLO, or fine-tune dials.
  7. Wait for an alert. ACT or WATCH tier alerts have [Buy 0.0X] buttons. Tap one.
  8. The bot buys via Jupiter, fires your TP/SL/TSL auto-exits, and DMs honest receipts.

How to use it

  • /trader — open the hub (balance, open positions, realized P&L)
  • /portfolio — see your open positions with live PnL
  • /history — closed trades + cumulative win/loss
  • /wallet — balance + pubkey
  • /closeall — panic exit every open position
  • /alert composite_score act — only ACT-tier signals (no WATCH/SCOUT)
  • Or use the persistent home buttons at the bottom of the chat.
