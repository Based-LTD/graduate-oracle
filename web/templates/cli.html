Pipe pump.fun graduation alerts into your bot.

Real-time graduation alert API for sniper bots and fast traders. Sign up, pay in SOL, stream from terminal. Zero install. Zero browser.

Install + sign up in one command
npx goracle signup builder
Requires Node 18+. The CLI mints a fresh API key, renders a Solana Pay QR in your terminal, and polls for confirmation. Pay with Phantom mobile or send manually. Your key is saved to ~/.config/goracle/config.json.

Use it

goracle accuracy

Live calibration receipt — no key required. 30-day hit rate on mints we score ≥70% in their first 60 seconds, lifetime number, weekly trajectory under the calibrated regime.

goracle probe <mint>

Score a single mint right now. grad_prob, current multiple, vSOL, buyer count, flags (bundled, manufactured, dex-paid, fee-delegated).

goracle live --threshold 0.70 --max-mult 2.0

Streaming table of every currently-tracked mint, filtered + sorted by confidence. Auto-refreshes every 2 seconds. --json for piping to jq.

goracle me

Show your saved key, tier, expiry, today's API call count.

goracle resume

Pick up a signup whose poll loop was interrupted. Safe to Ctrl-C mid-payment.

How payment works

The CLI does not hold your funds and does not ask for a private key. It POSTs to /api/upgrade, gets back a Solana Pay deeplink, renders it as a QR for your phone, and polls until the on-chain transaction confirms. Tier upgrade applies automatically.

Founding rate locks forever — your price never goes up at renewal, on the same tier and plan.
